RTL Rules\n255\t\n256\t- `dir=\"rtl\"` + `lang=\"fa\"` for Persian; `dir=\"ltr\"` + `lang=\"en\"` for English\n257\t- Sidebar **stays on the left** in both directions (Vitrea design decision)\n258\t- Logical CSS properties (`ms-`, `me-`, `ps-`, `pe-`, `text-align: start/end`)\n259\t- Directional icons mirror (chevrons, arrows); numbers stay LTR\n260\t- Persian dates use Jalali calendar; English dates use Gregorian\n261\t- Form labels and input text align to `start` in both directions\n262\t\n263\t---\n264\t\n265\t## 9.
